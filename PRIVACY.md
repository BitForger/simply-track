# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 18, 2026

Simply Track ("Simply Track," "we," or "the app") is a calorie-tracking app maintained by Bitforger. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

## Information the App Handles

Depending on the features you use, Simply Track may handle:

- Profile information, such as your age, biological sex, height, weight, activity level, nutrition goal, and weight-loss pace.
- Food and calorie entries, including food names, amounts, calorie values, dates, and times.
- App preferences, such as whether HealthKit synchronization, reminders, or burn-adjusted calorie targets are enabled.
- Health information made available through Apple Health, including dietary energy consumed and active energy burned, when you grant permission.

You choose what information to enter and which optional permissions to grant. The app does not require an account.

## How Information Is Used

The app uses this information only to provide its features, including:

- Calculating daily and weekly calorie targets.
- Recording and displaying food entries and progress.
- Synchronizing dietary calorie entries with Apple Health when enabled.
- Reading active energy burned from Apple Health when enabled, so burn-adjusted targets can be calculated.
- Scheduling local reminders when you enable reminders.

The app does not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale.

## Storage and Sharing

Simply Track stores its app data locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Based on the current app implementation, Simply Track does not operate a developer-controlled server, user account system, analytics service, or advertising SDK, and it does not send your personal or health information to Bitforger.

When you enable HealthKit features, information is exchanged with Apple Health according to the permissions you approve in Apple's HealthKit authorization prompt. The app may read dietary energy and active energy, and may write dietary energy entries to Apple Health. Apple handles HealthKit data under Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

Except for the Apple Health exchange you explicitly authorize, Simply Track does not share your information with other companies. The project may include links to third-party websites, including Ko-fi. Opening those links takes you outside the app and is subject to the third party's privacy policy and terms.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app requests notification permission and schedules a recurring local notification on your device. Reminder content is generated by the app and does not require sending your data to a notification server.

## Your Choices and Deletion

You can:

- Decline or revoke HealthKit permission in Apple's device settings.
- Disable reminders in the app's settings or device notification settings.
- Delete food entries using the app's controls.
- Delete the app's locally stored data by deleting the app from your device, subject to Apple's device behavior and backups.
- Manage or delete the app's HealthKit entries through the app and Apple Health.

Because the app does not maintain a developer-controlled account or server, Bitforger does not have a separate copy of your locally stored app data to retrieve or delete.

## Children's Privacy

Simply Track is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The latest version will be available in this repository with its effective date.

## Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Simply Track's privacy practices, please open an issue in the project repository or use the project's published support channel.
